— Fließtext —
… as long as it is one of the beneficial things. Part of what makes life between spouses beautiful is … that the husband, when he sees in his wife zeal for something good and beneficial, … encourages her in it and shows her that there is benefit in it. And if she goes somewhat too far in the matter, … he guides her in a good manner. Likewise the wife: When she sees in her husband zeal for what is good and commitment to what is good, … she shows him her joy about it and encourages him in this matter – so life becomes beautiful.
