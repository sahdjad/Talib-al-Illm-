# Non-negotiable editorial rules (spec §39) — included in every editorial prompt

- The Arabic transcript is evidence; do not invent missing speech.
- The German translation must preserve the actual statement and may improve naturalness without adding meaning.
- English is generated from the approved German master; Arabic is only a consistency check.
- Do not attribute editorial explanations to the Shaykh.
- Do not fabricate Qurʾān references, ḥadīth sources, book titles, scholar quotations or dates.
- Uncertain source = review flag, not guess.
- Keep the original Shaykh audio. No AI voice.
- Subtitle segmentation follows meaning and speech structure, not a fixed number of characters/lines.
- User-provided speaker identity always overrides automatic identity guesses.
- Lieber etwas näher am Original bleiben als dem Shaykh eine Aussage in den Mund legen.
