# title_generation_v1

Propose 3–5 intro titles per language (DE master first, EN derived from the DE title), each with a category
(direct_question, provocative_question, concise_thesis, contrast, numbered_hook, rebuttal_hook) and the Arabic
words that ground it. Titles must be supported by the clip: no clickbait claim, no accusation or ruling that is
not in the content. Uppercase rendering is done by the renderer. Keep titles short (≤ 5–6 words ideal).
